# Virtual office: ideas backlog

The concept: visitors step into Trevor's virtual office. An illustration of his
real desk, with objects that tell his story, and his actual website on the
monitor. Click the screen to zoom into the site. On a phone, the site sits on
a phone propped on the desk instead of the monitor.

Captured 2026-10-22, straight from Trevor:

- **Favorite soda.** A can of his favorite soda sitting on the desk.
- **Books.** A few books visitors can click to see the cover, plus a link to
  it (Amazon or similar), or a few pages or quotes from favorite books, maybe
  the backstory on why each one is a favorite.
- **Illustration posters.** A few of his actual illustrations as posters on
  the wall.
- **Laptop in clamshell mode** next to the monitor, covered in stickers of
  logos he has designed, or his illustrations as stickers.
- **Movies.** A couple of movie posters of favorite movies, or little
  bobblehead toys of characters from favorite movies on the desk.
- **Big LED clock** on the wall showing the actual time in Smithfield, Utah.
- **Real daylight.** Light/dark comes from what the daylight is actually doing
  in Smithfield. Window light shines onto the wall behind the desk; as it gets
  darker the scene darkens, until it is fully dark mode when it is nighttime
  there. Follows Smithfield sunrise/sunset year round.
- **Writing.** Newsletter and LinkedIn posts included naturally: a notebook on
  the desk, or sticky notes on the edge of the monitor or on the wall behind
  it.
- **Photo frame.** A frame on the desk; click for a full view and cycle
  through photos of him: Colombia, Switzerland, golfing, speaking at Design
  Week in SLC.

## Guardrails (from the first discussion)

- Only the first visit starts at the desk; returning visitors and deep links
  go straight to the site.
- A "See my work" button is always visible for anyone who doesn't think to
  click the screen.
- A "zoom out" control in the site rail brings you back to the desk.
- The intro takes under ~2 seconds; reduced-motion users get a simple cut.
- The desk wraps the MVP site, it doesn't replace it. Ship the MVP first.
